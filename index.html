01

How the parts fit

A harness is the app around an AI model. It gives the model tools, and you decide what it may do. Every kit follows the same assembly.

  1. Part A

    The model

    The AI that reads and writes. At UF you reach models through NaviGator AI Toolkit: local models on HiPerGator and approved cloud models.

  2. Part B

    The harness

    OpenCode, Pi, Cline, or Copilot. It lets the model read files, run commands, and search, and it asks for approval where you set it to.

  3. Part C

    Your configuration

    House rules in AGENTS.md, skills for your field, plugins you build, and permissions that match your data.
